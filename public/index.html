Filipe Nunes Vicente

Research Scientist · Cell Biology & Imaging · EMBL Heidelberg

I’m a cell biologist and biophysicist with 10 years of experience in cellular and molecular biology, advanced fluorescence microscopy, and quantitative imaging. Currently a postdoctoral researcher at EMBL Heidelberg in the Diz-Muñoz Lab, where I design synthetic molecular tools to control actin mechanics and build high-throughput imaging pipelines powered by machine learning.

Previously, I completed my PhD at IINS Bordeaux with Dr. Grégory Giannone, developing custom microfabricated devices to combine cell stretching with super-resolution microscopy and single-particle tracking.

Outside the lab, I make things at CoMaking Space — 3D printing, woodworking, and whatever breaks next. I care a lot about how science gets communicated, speak five languages at varying levels of embarrassment, and have a background in radio journalism that occasionally makes itself useful.

Research

I combine optogenetics, super-resolution microscopy, and quantitative image analysis to understand how cells regulate their mechanics. Currently at EMBL Heidelberg, I design synthetic actin crosslinkers and high-throughput imaging workflows to probe cytoskeletal organisation at the nanoscale.
