I'm a cell biologist and biophysicist with 10 years of experience in cellular and molecular biology, advanced fluorescence microscopy, and quantitative imaging. Currently a postdoctoral researcher at [EMBL Heidelberg](https://www.embl.org/groups/diz-munoz/) in the Diz-Muñoz Lab, where I design synthetic molecular tools to control actin mechanics and build high-throughput imaging pipelines powered by machine learning.

Previously, I completed my PhD at [IINS Bordeaux](https://www.iins.u-bordeaux.fr/GIANNONE) with Dr. Grégory Giannone, developing custom microfabricated devices to combine cell stretching with super-resolution microscopy and single-particle tracking.

Outside the lab, I make things at [CoMaking Space](https://comaking.de/) — 3D printing, woodworking, and whatever breaks next. I care a lot about how science gets communicated, speak five languages at varying levels of embarrassment, and have a background in radio journalism that occasionally makes itself useful.
